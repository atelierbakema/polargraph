The PolargraphSD has a case made of 16 laser cut acrylic parts. The case is not strictly necessary, but the touchscreen on the Polarshield is susceptible to a static electricity issue that makes it unresponsive. It's a temporary problem, but it's damn annoying and very hard to fix.

[![](https://c1.staticflickr.com/9/8682/16373121095_a2ce17416a.jpg)](https://flic.kr/p/qWQwti)

1. The parts can be got here from https://github.com/euphy/polargraph_models/blob/master/casev2/flat2/polargraph_case_parts_basic_v2.2.pdf, designed to be cut from 3mm material. The part are held together with 10mm long M3 bolts and nuts.



[![](https://c1.staticflickr.com/6/5754/24146014225_80125fa152.jpg)](https://flic.kr/p/CMGCN6)

2. Snap the first part into place. It's fragile, and is a snap-fit.



[![](https://c1.staticflickr.com/6/5824/23850371440_c8ed26416c.jpg)](https://flic.kr/p/CkzouG)

3. The part snapped on.


[![](https://c1.staticflickr.com/2/1504/23850371340_cdeafd208a.jpg)](https://flic.kr/p/CkzosY)

4. Added faceplate, driver plate and other LCD holder.





[![](https://c1.staticflickr.com/6/5759/24037917342_e2ba858613.jpg)](https://flic.kr/p/CC9BmJ)

5. Put this sub assembly together upside down, and introduce the board to it upside down too.




[![](https://c1.staticflickr.com/6/5824/24119914516_cc1d996485.jpg)](https://flic.kr/p/CKoSg7)

6. Slide the back into place. The metal tabs from the PSU jack are slightly long, so bend them over, or snip them off before adding this back part.






[![](https://c1.staticflickr.com/6/5781/24037913122_61c354fda8.jpg)](https://flic.kr/p/CC9A6Y)

7. M3 nuts go into the slots. I use tweezers to place them.



[![](https://c1.staticflickr.com/2/1692/23519219973_cc9e5c6f78.jpg)](https://flic.kr/p/BQj9GD)

8. The top plate goes on and holds most of the pieces together. Two 12mm M3 bolts (and washers) complete this stage.




[![](https://c1.staticflickr.com/6/5636/23517804654_f5dae6b343.jpg)](https://flic.kr/p/BQbTYA)

9. The part here has a notch on the top end, that a nut fits into.


[![](https://c1.staticflickr.com/2/1702/23850370790_18e240981a.jpg)](https://flic.kr/p/Ckzoiu)

10. More nuts and bolts to hold this end piece (as well as the base) on.


[![](https://c1.staticflickr.com/2/1471/23850370700_1a0de64391.jpg)](https://flic.kr/p/CkzogW)

11. A U-shaped piece surrounds the PSU jack on the Polarshield.
